Official Strategy Guide

POKEMON
LEAFGREEN

Nintendo Switch • 2026

01

Pallet Town

Your journey begins here. Oak stops you before you can leave — head to his lab to receive your starter. Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle. Your rival picks the one with a type advantage over yours.

Key Items
  • Starter Pokemon (from Oak)
  • Pokedex (from Oak after rival battle)
  • Town Map (from Daisy in rival's house)
Wild Pokemon
  • NoneN/A
Trainer Tips
  • Bulbasaur is the easiest pick — type advantages over Brock and Misty make the first two gyms straightforward.
  • Talk to everyone in Oak's lab before leaving to trigger the Pokedex gift on your return.
02

Route 1

A short grassy path between Pallet Town and Viridian City. Grind your starter to level 6–8 before moving on. An NPC near the start hands you a free Potion on your first pass.

Key Items
  • Potion (free from NPC on first trip north)
Wild Pokemon
  • PidgeyCommon
  • RattataCommon
Trainer Tips
  • Catch a Pidgey — it evolves into Pidgeot and remains a reliable flyer and attacker through the entire game.
  • Rattata is weak long-term but useful filler early if you need a second team slot quickly.
03

Viridian City

First city with a Pokemon Center and Mart. The Gym is locked until you earn all other badges. Deliver Oak's Parcel (buy it at the Mart) back to Oak in Pallet, then return — this unlocks Poke Ball purchases.

Key Items
  • Oak's Parcel (buy at Mart, deliver to Oak)
  • Poke Balls (available at Mart after delivery)
Wild Pokemon
  • None (city)N/A
Trainer Tips
  • Stock up on Potions and Poke Balls before heading north — Viridian Forest is long with no healing.
  • The Gym Leader here is Giovanni. You cannot challenge him until all 7 other badges are collected.
04

Route 2

Route 2 wraps around both sides of Viridian Forest. The southern stretch connects to Viridian City; the northern exit leads to Pewter City. A gatehouse midway through holds an NPC who will trade HM05 Flash — useful for Rock Tunnel later.

Key Items
  • HM05 Flash (from Oak's aide in gatehouse — requires 10 caught Pokemon)
  • Potion, Antidote (hidden in trees on both sides)
  • Ether (hidden, north section)
Wild Pokemon
  • PidgeyCommon
  • RattataCommon
  • Nidoran♀Rare
  • Nidoran♂Rare
Trainer Tips
  • Make sure you have 10 Pokemon registered in your Pokedex before passing through the gatehouse — the Flash HM is practically required for Rock Tunnel.
  • You can't access the north side of Route 2 (and its hidden items) until you have HM01 Cut much later.
05

Viridian Forest

A maze-like Bug-type forest packed with trainers. Pikachu appears at low odds — worth hunting if you want the series mascot. Several items are hidden in the grass and trees.

Key Items
  • Antidote (hidden)
  • Potion (hidden)
  • Poke Ball (hidden)
Wild Pokemon
  • CaterpieCommon
  • MetapodCommon
  • WeedleCommon
  • KakunaCommon
  • PikachuRare
Trainer Tips
  • Bug moves do nothing useful against Brock — Butterfree's Confusion (Normal/Psy) is your best bet if you evolve Caterpie.
  • Pikachu has a ~5% rate. Use Repel to suppress Caterpie/Weedle encounters while hunting it.
06

Pewter City — Gym Leader: Brock

Home of the first gym. Brock uses Rock/Ground types. Visit the Pewter Museum before leaving — a scientist in back gives you an Old Amber, which can be revived into Aerodactyl at Cinnabar Island much later.

Key Items
  • Boulder Badge (from Brock)
  • TM39 Rock Tomb (from Brock)
  • Old Amber (from Museum scientist — use Cut to reach him)
Brock's Team
  • Geodude Lv 12Rock/Gnd
  • Onix Lv 14Rock/Gnd
Trainer Tips
  • Water and Grass are 4x effective against Onix. Bulbasaur and Squirtle trivialize this fight.
  • Charmander players: grind Butterfree to Lv 12 for Confusion. It deals neutral damage and is your best realistic option.
  • Onix's Special Defense is very low despite its bulk. Always hit with special moves.
07

Route 3

A long trainer gauntlet running east from Pewter toward Mt. Moon. First chance to catch Jigglypuff. Both Nidoran genders are available here — Moon Stone evolutions make them strong end-game Pokemon.

Key Items
  • Various TMs and healing items from trainer battles
Wild Pokemon
  • SpearowCommon
  • SandshrewCommon
  • Nidoran♀Common
  • Nidoran♂Common
  • JigglypuffRare
Trainer Tips
  • Nidoking and Nidoqueen are among the best Pokemon in LeafGreen — catch a Nidoran pair here and save your Moon Stones.
  • Sandshrew is a LeafGreen exclusive. Its evolution Sandslash has great Defense and Slash — a solid physical attacker.
08

Mt. Moon

A two-floor cave with multiple Team Rocket encounters. The exit is guarded by a Super Nerd who forces you to choose one of two Fossils: Dome (Kabuto) or Helix (Omanyte). You can only take one.

Key Items
  • TM46 Thief (from Rocket Grunt)
  • Dome Fossil OR Helix Fossil (choose one at exit)
  • Moon Stone x2 (hidden on floor 2)
  • Escape Rope, Repel (hidden)
Wild Pokemon
  • ZubatCommon
  • GeodudeCommon
  • ParasRare
  • ClefairyVery Rare
Trainer Tips
  • Clefairy evolves with a Moon Stone into Clefable — one of the most versatile Normal-types in the game. Worth the hunt.
  • Helix → Omanyte (Water/Rock, better movepool). Dome → Kabuto (Rock/Water, better Speed/Attack). Both are good.
  • Use Repel on floor 2 to suppress Zubat encounters while hunting Clefairy near the upper ladders.
09

Route 4

A short route flanking Mt. Moon on both sides. The east end near the Pokemon Center has an NPC selling a Magikarp for 500P — a bargain for what evolves into Gyarados at level 20.

Key Items
  • Magikarp for 500P (from NPC inside the Pokemon Center)
  • TM05 Roar (hidden on west side)
Wild Pokemon
  • SpearowCommon
  • RattataCommon
  • SandshrewRare
Trainer Tips
  • Buy the Magikarp. 500P is trivial and Gyarados becomes one of the strongest Pokemon you'll have by mid-game.
  • Leave Magikarp in the Day-Care (Route 5) to level it painlessly toward evolution if you don't want to grind it yourself.
10

Cerulean City — Gym Leader: Misty

Second gym. Misty's Starmie is fast with high Special Attack. Before challenging her, visit Routes 24 & 25 to the north, beat Nugget Bridge, and collect the S.S. Anne Ticket from Bill at Sea Cottage. Track down the Rocket Grunt north of the city for TM28 Dig.

Key Items
  • Cascade Badge (from Misty)
  • TM03 Water Pulse (from Misty)
  • Bike Voucher (from man in house south of gym)
  • TM28 Dig (from Rocket Grunt near the gym)
Misty's Team
  • Staryu Lv 18Water
  • Starmie Lv 21Water/Psy
Trainer Tips
  • Grass and Electric wreck this gym. Pikachu from Viridian Forest was built for this fight.
  • Starmie can cause confusion with Water Pulse — bring multiple Pokemon and rotate if confused.
  • The Bike Voucher is redeemable at the Bike Shop in Vermilion for a free Bicycle. Don't skip it.
11

Routes 24 & 25 / Sea Cottage

North of Cerulean. Beat the five-trainer Nugget Bridge gauntlet on Route 24 (including a Rocket disguised as the sixth "trainer") then follow Route 25 east to Bill's Sea Cottage. Help Bill escape his Pokemon transformation to earn the S.S. Anne Ticket.

Key Items
  • Nugget (reward for Nugget Bridge — sell for 5000P)
  • S.S. Anne Ticket (from Bill at Sea Cottage)
  • TM45 Attract (from girl on Route 25)
Wild Pokemon
  • OddishCommon
  • AbraRare
  • SlowpokeRare
Trainer Tips
  • Abra Teleports on turn 1 — throw a Poke Ball immediately without attacking.
  • Slowpoke is a LeafGreen exclusive that evolves into Slowbro, a bulky Water/Psychic attacker — worth catching early.
  • Oddish is plentiful here; it evolves into Vileplume (via Leaf Stone) or Bellossom — solid Grass coverage for the rest of the game.
12

Route 5

Runs south from Cerulean City toward the Underground Path (a tunnel connecting to Route 6). The Day-Care Centre sits just off the route — drop off a Pokemon to level it passively while you walk.

Key Items
  • TM05 Roar (from Day-Care aide outside the centre)
  • Max Ether (hidden in grass)
Wild Pokemon
  • MeowthCommon
  • MankeyCommon
  • OddishCommon
  • AbraRare
Trainer Tips
  • Leave Magikarp in the Day-Care here and forget about it — by the time you reach Vermilion it may already be close to level 20 and ready to evolve into Gyarados.
  • Mankey evolves into Primeape at Lv 28 — a strong Fighting-type that stays useful all game.
13

Route 6

The south half of the Cerulean–Vermilion corridor. Exits from the Underground Path tunnel and feeds directly into the north gate of Vermilion City. Several trainers line the route for easy experience.

Key Items
  • Elixir (hidden in the tall grass)
Wild Pokemon
  • MeowthCommon
  • MankeyCommon
  • OddishCommon
  • PsyduckRare
Trainer Tips
  • Psyduck is rare here but it's a LeafGreen exclusive that evolves into Golduck — a reliable Water-type if you don't want to use Gyarados or Starmie.
  • Aim for level 20–22 before boarding the S.S. Anne so your team can handle the trainer density on the ship.
14

Vermilion City / S.S. Anne — Gym Leader: Lt. Surge

Port city with the S.S. Anne docked in the harbor. Board the ship to battle trainers across many cabins, find items, and get HM01 Cut from the Captain. Use Cut to trim the bush blocking the Gym entrance. Lt. Surge's Raichu is fast — a Ground-type counters it completely.

Key Items
  • HM01 Cut (from S.S. Anne Captain)
  • Thunder Badge (from Lt. Surge)
  • TM34 Shock Wave (from Lt. Surge)
  • Bicycle (redeem Bike Voucher at the Bike Shop)
  • Old Rod (from Fisherman near the gym)
  • Multiple TMs and healing items on the S.S. Anne
Lt. Surge's Team
  • Voltorb Lv 21Electric
  • Pikachu Lv 18Electric
  • Raichu Lv 24Electric
Trainer Tips
  • Ground-types are immune to all Electric moves. Sandshrew (Route 3) or Diglett from Diglett's Cave south of the city make this gym trivial.
  • The trash-can switch puzzle is randomized each attempt. Search cans in adjacent pairs — both switches are always near each other.
  • The S.S. Anne leaves permanently after you get Cut. Loot every cabin before talking to the Captain.
15

Route 11

Runs east from Vermilion toward Route 12. Blocked by a sleeping Snorlax on Route 12 — you'll need the Poke Flute to pass later. The upper gatehouse has an Oak aide who gives you an item for catching enough Pokemon.

Key Items
  • Item Finder (from Oak's aide in upper gatehouse — requires 30 caught Pokemon)
Wild Pokemon
  • SpearowCommon
  • EkansCommon
  • DrowzeeRare
Trainer Tips
  • Ekans is a LeafGreen exclusive — Arbok has decent stats and access to useful moves. Worth a catch for the Pokedex.
  • Drowzee evolves into Hypno, a solid Psychic-type. It's rarer but worth hunting if you don't have a Psychic-type yet.
  • You can't progress east past the Snorlax on Route 12 yet — head back and go north toward Lavender Town instead.
16

Lavender Town

The eerie ghost-type town built around Pokemon Tower, a graveyard for fallen Pokemon. Team Rocket has seized the tower and a ghost blocks the top floors. You need the Silph Scope (from Celadon's Rocket Hideout) to reveal the ghost — it's a Marowak.

Key Items
  • Silph Scope required to reveal ghost (get from Rocket Hideout in Celadon first)
  • Poke Flute (from Mr. Fuji on Pokemon Tower 7F)
  • Escape Rope, Revive (hidden in Pokemon Tower)
Wild Pokemon (Pokemon Tower)
  • GastlyCommon
  • HaunterRare
  • CuboneRare
Trainer Tips
  • You can't complete Pokemon Tower until you get the Silph Scope — head to Celadon first, clear the Rocket Hideout, then return.
  • Haunter is a LeafGreen exclusive and an excellent Ghost/Poison attacker. Catch it with the Silph Scope equipped.
  • The Marowak ghost cannot be caught — do not waste Poke Balls. Defeat it to proceed.
  • The Poke Flute is your key to waking the Snorlax blocking Routes 12 and 16.
17

Rock Tunnel / Routes 9 & 10

Route 9 runs east from Cerulean toward the Rock Tunnel entrance on Route 10. Rock Tunnel is a two-floor pitch-dark cave — bring Flash or budget for a lot of fumbling. Route 10's south section leads to Lavender Town.

Key Items
  • HM05 Flash (Route 2 aide — needed here)
  • Repel x5+ (highly recommended)
  • Revive, Escape Rope (hidden in tunnel)
Wild Pokemon
  • ZubatCommon
  • GeodudeCommon
  • MachopRare
  • OnixRare
Trainer Tips
  • Flash lowers enemy accuracy to near-zero and makes the tunnel far less punishing. Get it from the Route 2 aide before entering.
  • Machop evolves into Machoke and then Machamp — one of the best Fighting-types and worth the effort to find.
  • Stock Repels before entering. Zubat encounters every few steps without them will drain your PP quickly.
18

Celadon City — Gym Leader: Erika

The shopping hub of Kanto. The Celadon Department Store sells nearly every evolution stone and TM in the game. The Team Rocket Game Corner hides a basement hideout where you fight Giovanni for the Silph Scope. Erika's Gym uses pure Grass types.

Key Items
  • Rainbow Badge (from Erika)
  • TM19 Giga Drain (from Erika)
  • Silph Scope (from Giovanni in Rocket Hideout)
  • Eevee (free from top floor of Celadon Mansion)
  • Evolution Stones (at Department Store)
  • TM02, TM07, TM12, TM36 (Department Store)
Erika's Team
  • Victreebel Lv 29Grass/Psn
  • Tangela Lv 24Grass
  • Vileplume Lv 29Grass/Psn
Trainer Tips
  • Fire, Ice, Flying, and Poison all wreck Erika's team. Charmander players finally have an easy gym.
  • Clear the Rocket Hideout before challenging Erika — you need the Silph Scope to finish Pokemon Tower in Lavender Town.
  • Eevee from the Mansion roof can be evolved with stones from the store into Vaporeon, Jolteon, or Flareon — all strong choices.
19

Routes 16, 17 & 18

The western cycling road running from Celadon south toward Fuchsia City. Route 16 has a sleeping Snorlax blocking access — wake it with the Poke Flute. The Cycling Road (Route 17) is fast on the Bicycle and packed with bikers worth good EXP.

Key Items
  • HM02 Fly (from girl in house west of Snorlax on Route 16)
  • Rare Candy (hidden on Route 17)
  • TM50 Overheat (hidden on Route 17)
Wild Pokemon
  • DoduoCommon
  • RattataCommon
  • FearowRare
Trainer Tips
  • HM02 Fly is one of the most important HMs in the game — teaches your flyer to teleport back to any city instantly.
  • The Snorlax here (Lv 30) can be caught with enough Poke Balls after wearing it down. It's a superb tank for your team.
  • Doduo evolves into Dodrio at Lv 31 — a fast Normal/Flying physical attacker, solid if you need a second flyer.
20

Fuchsia City / Safari Zone — Gym Leader: Koga

Home of the Safari Zone — the only place to catch Tauros, Kangaskhan, Scyther (FireRed) / Pinsir (LeafGreen), and Chansey. Navigate the Zone to retrieve the Gold Teeth, which you trade to the Warden for HM04 Strength. Koga's gym uses invisible walls and Poison types.

Key Items
  • Soul Badge (from Koga)
  • TM27 Return (from Koga)
  • HM04 Strength (from Safari Zone Warden — trade Gold Teeth)
  • HM03 Surf (in Safari Zone Area 3 house)
  • Gold Teeth (found in Safari Zone)
Safari Zone Pokemon
  • Pinsir (LG)Rare
  • TaurosRare
  • KangaskhanRare
  • ChanseyVery Rare
Trainer Tips
  • HM03 Surf is in the Safari Zone and is mandatory for progression — don't leave without it.
  • Pinsir is a LeafGreen exclusive with great Attack. Throw Bait to make it easier to catch, then toss Balls.
  • Koga uses Smokescreen and self-destruct. Bring Antidotes and don't lead with a weak Pokemon.
  • Koga's Team: Koffing Lv 37, Muk Lv 39, Koffing Lv 37, Weezing Lv 43. Ground types clean this gym.
21

Routes 12, 13, 14 & 15

A long coastal loop connecting Lavender Town south to Fuchsia City. Route 12 has a second sleeping Snorlax — wake it with the Poke Flute. These routes are heavy with Fishers and Bird Keepers; good grinding stretch before the back half of the game.

Key Items
  • Good Rod (from Fisherman on Route 12)
  • TM11 Sunny Day (Route 13, hidden)
  • Various Berries from Berry trees along all routes
Wild Pokemon
  • VenonatCommon
  • GloomCommon
  • DittoRare
  • Scyther (FR) / Pinsir (LG)Very Rare
Trainer Tips
  • The second Snorlax (Route 12) is also Lv 30 and catchable — useful if you missed the first one on Route 16.
  • Ditto is a handy utility Pokemon for breeding later. It's rare on these routes but worth grabbing for Pokedex completion.
  • The Good Rod unlocks fishing for Poliwag and Krabby — both useful if you need Water coverage.
22

Routes 19 & 20

The open-water Surfing routes south of Fuchsia City leading to Cinnabar Island. Long stretches of ocean filled with Tentacool. The Seafoam Islands sit mid-route — a two-entrance cave where Articuno lives deep inside.

Key Items
  • Articuno (legendary — deep in Seafoam Islands)
  • Various items hidden in Seafoam Islands
Wild Pokemon
  • TentacoolCommon
  • TentacruelRare
  • HorseaRare (fishing)
Trainer Tips
  • Tentacruel has surprising bulk and solid Special stats — it can carry you through water routes if you don't have a better Water-type.
  • Articuno (Lv 50) in Seafoam Islands is catchable now — bring Ultra Balls, weaken it carefully, and save before the encounter.
  • Seafoam Islands requires Strength and Surf to fully navigate. Push the boulders into holes in the correct order to block the underground currents.
23

Cinnabar Island / Pokémon Mansion — Gym Leader: Blaine

Volcanic island with a Research Lab that revives Fossils and the Pokemon Mansion — a burned-out building where you find the Secret Key to unlock Blaine's Gym. The Lab also gives you a free Hitmonchan. Blaine uses Fire types.

Key Items
  • Volcano Badge (from Blaine)
  • TM38 Fire Blast (from Blaine)
  • Secret Key (in Pokemon Mansion — unlocks Gym)
  • Hitmonchan (free from dojo in the Research Lab area)
  • Fossil revival (bring Dome/Helix/Old Amber to the Lab)
Blaine's Team
  • Growlithe Lv 42Fire
  • Ponyta Lv 40Fire
  • Rapidash Lv 42Fire
  • Arcanine Lv 47Fire
Trainer Tips
  • Water and Rock obliterate this gym. Gyarados, Vaporeon, or your Fossil Pokemon (Omastar / Kabutops) are ideal.
  • Magmar is a LeafGreen exclusive found in the Pokemon Mansion — a strong Fire-type if you don't have one.
  • Get your Fossils revived here before moving on — Aerodactyl (from Old Amber) is a fast Rock/Flying attacker worth having.
  • Hitmonchan is a LeafGreen exclusive dojo gift. Strong Punch-move coverage makes it a great team addition.
24

Route 21

A short Surf route between Cinnabar Island and Pallet Town. Tangela appears here — one of the few places in the game to catch it. Mostly used as a fast travel route to Pallet and Oak's lab for a quick storyline checkpoint.

Key Items
  • No major story items — minor hidden items in grass patches
Wild Pokemon
  • TentacoolCommon
  • TentacruelRare
  • TangelaRare
Trainer Tips
  • Tangela has surprisingly good Defense and learns Bind, Mega Drain, and Sleep Powder — viable Grass-type if you skipped Oddish.
  • Fly north to Pallet Town and visit Oak after getting all 7 badges to advance the Saffron City storyline.
25

Saffron City / Silph Co. — Gym Leader: Sabrina

Saffron is locked by Team Rocket until you fight through the 11-floor Silph Co. headquarters and defeat Giovanni on the top floor. After liberating the building you receive a free Lapras. Then challenge Sabrina's Psychic gym — her Alakazam is extremely fast and hits hard.

Key Items
  • Marsh Badge (from Sabrina)
  • TM04 Calm Mind (from Sabrina)
  • Lapras (free from employee after clearing Silph Co.)
  • Master Ball (from Silph Co. president after beating Giovanni)
  • TM26 Earthquake (from Giovanni in Silph Co.)
Sabrina's Team
  • Kadabra Lv 38Psychic
  • Mr. Mime Lv 37Psychic
  • Venomoth Lv 38Bug/Psn
  • Alakazam Lv 43Psychic
Trainer Tips
  • Save the Master Ball for the roaming legendary (Raikou/Entei/Suicune in post-game) or an emergency. Do not waste it on a static encounter.
  • Dark and Ghost types are immune or resistant to Psychic. Haunter/Gengar destroy this gym.
  • Silph Co. has teleport pads that crisscross floors — the Card Key (found on 5F) is needed to open locked doors throughout the building.
26

Routes 22 & 23

Route 22 begins just west of Viridian City — your rival intercepts you here early in the game. Route 23 is the final stretch to Victory Road, lined with badge-check gates. All 8 Kanto badges are required to pass through. Good last-chance training spot.

Key Items
  • All 8 Gym Badges required to pass Route 23's gates
  • Max Potion, PP Up (hidden in Route 23 grass)
Wild Pokemon
  • MankeyCommon (Rt 22)
  • Nidoran♀/♂Common (Rt 22)
  • FearowCommon (Rt 23)
  • EkansRare (Rt 23)
Trainer Tips
  • Your rival battle on Route 22 (early game) uses your starter's weakness — come back here later to battle him again for lore and experience.
  • Aim for level 45–48 across your full team before entering Victory Road. The Elite Four will punish underprepared teams.
27

Victory Road

A three-floor cave dungeon that serves as the final test before the Elite Four. Strength is required to push boulders onto switches and open the path. Moltres (Lv 50) waits near the end — the only chance to catch it in the wild.

Key Items
  • Moltres (legendary, Lv 50 — one encounter only)
  • TM43 Secret Power (hidden)
  • Full Restore, Max Revive (hidden)
Wild Pokemon
  • Zubat / GolbatCommon
  • Geodude / GravelerCommon
  • MachokeRare
  • OnixRare
Trainer Tips
  • Save before approaching Moltres. Bring Ultra Balls and weaken it carefully — it's a Fire/Flying legendary and a great team member.
  • Repel through Victory Road unless you're still grinding. The dungeon is long and trainer-dense.
  • Stock Full Restores and Max Revives before entering the Plateau. You cannot return to heal between Elite Four battles.
28

Indigo Plateau — Elite Four & Champion

The final challenge. The Elite Four must be faced in strict order with no healing between rooms — stock Full Restores, Max Revives, and Elixirs before entering. After defeating all four, your rival awaits as the Champion.

Elite Four Order
  • Lorelei (Ice) — Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel counter her
  • Bruno (Fighting) — Psychic, Flying, Fairy counter him
  • Agatha (Ghost/Poison) — Dark, Ghost, Ground counter her
  • Lance (Dragon) — Ice is 4x effective; Dragon and Fairy work too
  • Rival (Champion) — mixed team; type coverage is key
Recommended Levels
  • Minimum viableLv 48+
  • ComfortableLv 52+
  • OverleveledLv 58+
Trainer Tips
  • LeafGreen exclusives shine here: Starmie (Water/Psychic) handles Lorelei, Bruno, and Agatha; Slowbro soaks hits while dishing Psychic damage; Pinsir's X-Scissor tears through Lance's Dragon types.
  • Lance's Dragonite trio all know Hyper Beam — switch in a resistant Pokemon after each Hyper Beam (it forces a recharge turn) to attack safely.
  • Agatha uses Confuse Ray and Destiny Bond constantly — lead with your strongest attacker and try to one-shot her Pokemon before status piles up.
  • Your rival's team is based on your starter. Expect a full 6-Pokemon team in the mid-to-high 50s. This is the hardest fight in the game.
  • You can re-challenge the Elite Four after beating the game — best source of late-game EXP for completing your Pokedex.
